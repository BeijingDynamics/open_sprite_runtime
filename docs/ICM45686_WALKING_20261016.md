Motor command/feedback caps and differential transforms remain those of the
qualified baseline. Host ankle joint gains are not direct motor-side MIT gains.
The generated native parameter bundle was checked byte-for-byte against baseline;
old gain multipliers must not scale the effective values a second time.

ICM uses mode-3 SPI requested at 1 MHz, 800 Hz ODR, 4g/2000 deg/s ranges,
INT1 DRDY timestamps, and the existing six-axis estimator. The mounting rotation
is body = (-sensorZ, -sensorY, -sensorX). Startup estimates gyro bias during
15 seconds of stillness. Gyro observations are raw minus bias with the mounting
rotation; gravity comes from the software estimator. No magnetic heading fusion.
SPIMU003 preserves ICM raw-count scales and sensor-frame values for the existing
policy adapter; the display's body-frame quaternion must not be rotated twice.

Policy inference remains native ONNX Runtime CPU at 50 Hz. The CAN scheduler is
2 kHz, ankle host PD and four ankle motors are 500 Hz, and other motors are 50 Hz.

- Fake-peer powered actor: 2001 frames, byte-exact parity and transmitted-target

  75 ms and 36 ms respectively, including test/process teardown.
- Final 8-second zero-gain startup preflight passed. Left ankle pitch projection
  remained advisory, as in the baseline; command mechanical limits remained active.

## Site commands and operational sequence

These commands describe the qualified Jetson installation, not a fresh-clone
installer. They require the sealed `upper_body_pd_wrist7_20261014` baseline,
its model/calibrations/qualification evidence, and the generated
`icm_native_20261016` candidate. Missing evidence must fail closed.
Generation tools record the exact site inputs; date-stamped evidence paths are
intentional commissioning dependencies, not downloadable data supplied here.

```bash
cd /home/tony/open_sprite_runtime
bash run_sprite0825_icm_native_walk40_on_253.sh --check
sudo bash run_sprite0825_icm_native_walk40_on_253.sh --preflight-only
```

Only after fresh onsite approval, battery supply, fall arrest, clear space and
the power safety operator are ready:

```bash
sudo bash run_sprite0825_icm_native_walk40_on_253.sh ENABLE_ICM45686_WALK40
```

The first 15 seconds of stillness and zero-gain preflight precede the powered
40-second timeline. Powered startup holds for 1 second and ramps gains over
5 seconds. Timeline: 0-10 s stand; 10-13 s ramp to 0.10 m/s; 13-27 s forward;
27-30 s ramp to zero; 30-40 s stand, then disable verification. Faults abort
earlier. Zero commanded velocity alone does not prove stable standing.

The wrapper temporarily forces SPI runtime power management on and restores the
previous setting on exit. Native acquisition is intentionally terminated when
the wrapper ends: capture `completed:false` alone is not a walking failure;
check actor/controller completion and all-motor disable evidence.

The BNO entry remains unchanged for rollback. Do not run two sensor readers or
two controllers simultaneously. No new zero-position calibration is performed.

## Development lessons retained

The module's four series resistors were initially mistaken for pull-ups and
removed, disconnecting the SPI signals. WHO_AM_I recovered after the module was
repaired. Check connectivity before attributing all-zero reads to SPI software.
SPI runtime PM produced measurable read-latency spikes; the bounded PM wrapper
is part of this candidate. Requested SPI clock readback is not an oscilloscope
measurement. An initial ONNX shared-library SONAME-link omission was fixed;
preflight now checks loader dependencies and reports child failures directly.
