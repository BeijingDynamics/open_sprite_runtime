`model2999` remains the rollback policy until hardware shadow mode is complete.

- Exact 31-motor CAN/channel mapping, firmware ranges, signs, and zero offsets
- Independently measured soft and hard limits
- Left/right differential-ankle matrices and motor zeros
- IMU mounting transform, timestamp behavior, and validation limits
- Physical emergency-stop chain and watchdog behavior
- Thermal/current margin and 4340P clip-boundary behavior on hardware

No CAN transmit backend is present. Do not arm from the example configuration.
